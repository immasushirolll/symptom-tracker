💛 About This App

A few things to know about your Health Journal.

🔒 Your privacy matters

All your recordings are saved only on this computer. Nothing is uploaded to the internet. Your health information stays completely private and in your hands.

🌿 What is this app for?

This Health Journal helps you build a habit of checking in with yourself each week. By talking through how you've been feeling — your sleep, energy, mood, and health — you create a personal record over time.

You can share these recordings with your doctor, family member, or carer, or simply listen back yourself to notice patterns and progress.

🎙️ Tips for a good check-in

  • Find a quiet spot where you won't be interrupted
  • Speak naturally — there's no wrong way to do it
  • Aim for 2–5 minutes, but any length is fine
  • Try to check in around the same time each week
  • Be honest — this is just for you!

📞 Need help?

If you have any trouble using the app, ask a family member or trusted person to help you. This app is designed to be as simple as possible — just tap the big button and talk!

⚠️ Important note

This app is a personal health diary, not a medical tool. It does not give medical advice or contact emergency services. If you feel unwell or are in distress, please call your doctor or emergency services.
